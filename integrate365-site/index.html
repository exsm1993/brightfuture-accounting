    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Anything else? Book a demo and ask us directly.

    What is Integrate365?

    Integrate365 is an AI team that works inside Initiate365, the CRM for home-services companies. It follows up leads, confirms appointments, keeps projects moving and handles paperwork, on its own computer where you can watch every click.

    How does it learn a new task?

    Press Show it once and do the task the way your best person does it. Talk it through if you like. The agent pauses, records each click as a step and turns it into a skill draft. It only uses the skill after you or an admin approve it.

    Will it message my customers on its own?

    No. Skills that touch a customer or money always wait for a person's OK. When it needs to text someone, it drafts the message and waits for approval.

    What happens to our data?

    Skills carry the method only: how the work is done. Your logins, customers, prices and conversations never leave your workspace, and nothing from another company ever comes in. Passwords you type while teaching are never saved in a skill.

    Can I see what it's doing?

    Yes. Watch it work shows the agent's own computer live, what it's doing right now, and a timestamped list of everything it did, newest first.

    Who can approve a new skill?

    You or an admin. Every skill learned from a Show it once session stays a draft until someone approves it, and you can turn any skill on or off at any time.
