## 1. Why @timeinvestors works (steal these mechanics)

Time Investors isn't winning on luck — it runs a repeatable formula. These are the levers to copy:

1. **A locked visual template.** Every graphic has the same logo placement, the same teal accent color, the same bold condensed font. It's instantly recognizable in-feed and survives being reposted/screenshotted. This is the #1 growth lever.
2. **Curiosity-gap authority headlines.** "Study finds that…", "A UCLA neuroscientist discovered that…", "New study finds that…". Authority + surprise + relatability in one line.
3. **Tag-a-friend / send-this topics.** Relationships, sleep, parenting, health, mindset. People *share* these — shares drive reach far more than likes.
4. **"Read the caption for full details."** The graphic sells the click; the caption delivers value. This pumps saves, comments, and dwell time (the metrics the algorithm rewards).
5. **Carousels + singles mix.** Multi-slide posts hold attention longer and get more saves.
6. **A coherent-but-broad niche.** "Business | Mindset & Healthy Lifestyle" is wide enough for mass appeal but consistent enough to build an identity.

Your job: apply this exact machine to a niche your handle already implies.

## 5. The headline formulas (your engine)

Copy these frameworks, swap in your niche. Put the accent color on the **bold** words.

- **The Study Hook:** "New study finds that **[surprising claim about people/relationships]**"
- **The Authority Hook:** "A **[Stanford/Harvard/UCLA] [expert]** discovered that **[counterintuitive truth]**"
- **The Two-List Hook:** "Show me **[X]** and I'll show you **[Y]**"
- **The Callout Hook:** "**Ages 2 to 4** don't break children. They break **parents**" → yours: "**Your 20s** don't build your career. They build your **network**"
- **The Secret Hook:** "The **one thing** powerful people never do in a room full of strangers"
- **The Number Hook:** "**3 people** you need in your corner before you turn 30"

## 9. Growth tactics (first 90 days)

- **Reels first for reach.** Turn your top graphics into 7–15s reels (Ken Burns zoom on the image + trending audio + the headline as on-screen text). Static graphics build brand; Reels bring strangers.
- **Repost-friendly design.** Because your logo is baked in, every screenshot/repost is free advertising. Design for the screenshot.
- **Engage 20 min/day** on 5–10 similar big pages (comment early with something clever) to borrow their audience.
- **Story polls & questions daily** — cheap engagement that keeps you in the algorithm.
- **Hook-test on Reels:** if the first 1.5 seconds don't stop the scroll, remake it.
- **Double down ruthlessly.** When one post outperforms 5×, make three variations of it.
